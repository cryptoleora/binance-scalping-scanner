📊 COIN SIGNAL LOGIC

1️⃣ RSI

RSI below 40 = potential recovery zone.
RSI 40–60 = neutral.
RSI above 60 = stronger momentum.
RSI above 70 = overheating risk.

2️⃣ EMA TREND

Price above EMA20 and EMA55 = bullish structure.
EMA20 above EMA55 = stronger bullish trend.
Price below both = bearish structure.

3️⃣ VOLUME

Volume ratio compares current volume with the recent average. Higher volume gives stronger confirmation to the move.

4️⃣ BTC STRENGTH

Coin 24H performance is compared with BTC. Positive relative strength means the coin is outperforming BTC.

5️⃣ FINAL SCORE

Score ≥ 75 → BUY
Score 40–74 → WAIT
Score < 40 → AVOID
Data sources: Binance public market data, CoinGecko market-cap data, Alternative.me Fear & Greed, DeFiLlama stablecoin data.
Technical signals are model outputs, not guaranteed future price predictions. Funding/OI should be interpreted together with BTC price and trend.
