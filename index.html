📊 Coin Signal Logic
RSI
40–68 = healthier momentum zone.
>75 = overheated risk.
<30 = deeply weak / possible rebound zone.
EMA STRUCTURE
Price > EMA20 > EMA55 = bullish structure.
Price < EMA20 < EMA55 = bearish structure.
Anything between = mixed.
VOLUME
Volume >1.5× average = strong confirmation.
Volume <0.6× average = weak participation.
BTC RELATIVE STRENGTH
Coin 24H performance is compared against BTC. Positive = outperforming BTC. Negative = underperforming BTC.
SCORE
Trend + RSI + volume + BTC relative strength + market conditions combine into a 0–100 score.
SIGNAL
75+ = BUY setup
40–74 = WAIT
Below 40 = AVOID
